Overview

Sports Rag lets one account both buy and sell collectibles. Every listing goes to an admin for review before it appears in the store.

I built the shopping cart and the mock checkout flow, including the order history customers see after they buy.

What I built

  • User sign-up and login with Devise
  • Admin review queue for submitted products
  • Shopping cart management
  • Mock checkout and an order history page
