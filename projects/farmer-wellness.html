Overview

Farmers log in securely, fill out a wellness and stress questionnaire as often as they want, and track their scores over time.

Each result maps to a green, yellow, or red tier with matching resources. Red-tier results show crisis support such as the 988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

What I built

  • Separate client and server architecture, with Django on the back end
  • No third-party AI services, to keep sensitive answers private
  • Team workflow: user stories become GitHub issues, one branch per issue, tests and docs required, peer-reviewed pull requests
  • Will be hosted and maintained by the client's developers after handoff
